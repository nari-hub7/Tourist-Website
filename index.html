Welcome to Eastern Samar

Eastern Samar is located in the Eastern Visayas Region of the Philippines. Known as the "Land of Good Harbors" and Eastern Gateway of the Philippines," it is famous for its stunning natural attractions, historical landmarks, surfing destinations, and friendly Waray people.

Whether you love nature, history, adventure, or local culture, Eastern Samar has something for everyone.
