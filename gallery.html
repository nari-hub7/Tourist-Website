Did You Know?

🌅 Golden Sunrise

Eastern Samar is known as the "Land of the Golden Sunrise" because it is among the first places in the Philippines to witness the sunrise.

🏄 Surfing Paradise

Calicoan Island is one of the country's famous surfing destinations, visited by both local and international surfers.

⛪ Rich History

The province is home to centuries-old churches and historical landmarks, including the famous Balangiga Bells.
