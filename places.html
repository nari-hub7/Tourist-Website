Tourist Destinations

Discover the beauty and history of Eastern Samar.

Canhugas Nature Park

Located in Hernani, It's an absolute nature's of art, for centuries gigantic waves crushing and hammering the rocks and forming a natural made pool facing the pacific ocean.

Balangiga Bells

The Balangiga Bells symbolize the bravery and history of the Filipino people. They were returned to the Philippines in 2018 after being taken to the United States for more than a century.

Linao Cave

Linao Cave in Guiuan is known for its crystal-clear blue water and natural cave formations, making it a favorite destination for adventure seekers.

Borongan Cathedral

The Borongan Cathedral is one of the oldest churches in Eastern Samar and serves as an important religious landmark.

Sulangan Church

This famous pilgrimage site attracts thousands of devotees every year because of the miraculous image of St. Anthony of Padua.

Calicoan Island

Calicoan Island is one of the best surfing destinations in the Philippines. It also features white sand beaches and beautiful rock formations.
