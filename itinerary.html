3-Day Travel Itinerary

Explore the beauty of Eastern Samar in three unforgettable days.

📅 Day 1 – Nature Trip

🌄 Morning

Start your journey by visiting Canhugas Nature Park in Hernani. Enjoy the fresh mountain breeze, waves, and take memorable photos with family and friends.

🍽 Lunch

Taste local Waray delicacies at nearby restaurants before continuing your adventure.

🌅 Afternoon

Walk around the park, relax beside nature, and appreciate the peaceful environment before returning to your hotel.

📅 Day 2 – History of Balangiga

🌄 Morning

Travel to Balangiga and visit the famous Balangiga Bells, one of the most important historical landmarks in the Philippines.

⛪ Visit the Church

Explore San Lorenzo de Martir Church and learn about the history of the Balangiga Encounter.

🍽 Dinner

Enjoy local seafood dishes while exploring the beautiful town plaza.

📅 Day 3 – Guiuan Adventure

🌄 Morning

Visit the beautiful Linao Cave and enjoy its crystal-clear waters.

🏄 Afternoon

Head to Calicoan Island, known for surfing, white sand beaches, and amazing ocean views.

⛪ Evening

Before going home, stop by the famous Sulangan Church to offer a prayer and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.

🎒 Travel Tips

☀️ Best Time to Visit

November to May offers sunny weather and calmer seas.

🧴 Bring

Sunscreen, drinking water, extra clothes, camera, and comfortable shoes.

📸 Don't Forget

Capture photos of the sunrise, beaches, caves, churches, and local delicacies.
