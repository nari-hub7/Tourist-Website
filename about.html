About Eastern Samar

Learn more about the Province of the Golden Sunrise.

Province Profile

Eastern Samar is a province in Eastern Visayas, Philippines. It is famous for its breathtaking beaches, caves, waterfalls, historical landmarks, surfing destinations, and rich Waray culture.

The province consists of one city, twenty-two municipalities, and hundreds of barangays. Tourism, agriculture, and fishing are among the major sources of livelihood.

Eastern Samar at a Glance

Information Details
Region Eastern Visayas (Region VIII)
Capital Borongan City
Language Waray-Waray
Known For Golden Sunrise, Surfing, Historical Churches
Famous Tourist Spots Canhugas Nature Park, Balangiga Bells, Linao Cave, Calicoan Island
Best Time to Visit November to May
